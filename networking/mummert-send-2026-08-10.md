## Why the email is shaped this way

- **The ask is 15 minutes and a reaction, not adoption.** A coordinator who won the Spira teaching
  award (Apr 2019) is selected on caring about instruction — the email asks for his judgment, which
  is the ask that flatters truthfully and is easy to grant.
- **The permission question is load-bearing, not decorative.** STICK ingests past exams; doing that
  at scale without instructor buy-in is a real exposure. Asking him first converts the risk into
  the relationship.
- **No demo link in the first email.** The deployed app has dev credentials in front of it and
  nothing on the path is spec-grade yet (STATE.md's own caveat). "Show you the working tool" in
  person keeps control of the demo and gives the meeting a reason to exist.
- **12/12 claim is stated exactly as verified** — extraction matched the instructor key on one
  exam. Not "AI-verified," not "99% accurate." He teaches math; overclaiming to him is fatal.
