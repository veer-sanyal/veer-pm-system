# STICK demo protocol — first-user sessions

Reusable script for showing STICK to a human (friend demo, Sunkula meeting, first
pilot students). Goal is INSIGHT + COMMITMENT, not applause. Written 2026-08-20 for
the visiting-friend demo (closes `stick-first-user-shown`); reuse for every early demo.

## The one-liner (say this, then stop talking)

> "It's Duolingo, but for your actual college exams."

Backup, if they need one more sentence:
> "You give it the course. It tells you exactly what to practice today so the exam
> can't surprise you."

Do NOT explain more than that before they touch it. The gap between what you say and
what they think it does is the most valuable data in the session.

## Session order (20 min)

**1. Before showing anything (5 min): their life, not your product.**
- "Walk me through how you studied for your last hard exam. Like, the actual week."
- "What sucked most about it?"
- "What did you actually open the night before? Quizlet? Chegg? ChatGPT? Notes?"
- "Did you pay for anything? Would you have?"
Write their exact words down. Past behavior only; no hypotheticals yet.

**2. Hand them the keyboard (10 min): one-liner, then silence.**
- Say the one-liner. Log them into the seeded account. Then SHUT UP and take notes.
- Every place they hesitate, misclick, or ask "what's this?" is a finding. Do not
  rescue them for at least 10 seconds; the rescue destroys the data.
- At ~1 minute in: "What do you think this does?" (tests whether the product
  communicates itself; their answer is future marketing copy, verbatim)
- When they finish a question: "What would you do next?" (tests the loop's pull)

**3. After (5 min): the commitment test.**
- "Would you use this for a real class this fall? Which one?"
- If yes → THE ASK: **"If I set it up for that class, send me the syllabus tonight."**
  A yes-plus-syllabus is a real signal. "Yeah this is cool" with no syllabus is a NO
  and gets recorded as one.
- "What's missing that would stop you from using it the week before an exam?"
- "Who's the hardest-studying person you know? Would you send this to them?" (if yes,
  watch whether they actually do it, unprompted, within 48h)

## Log after every session (append below, dated)

- Their exact words describing the product back to you
- Top 3 stuck points (screen + what they expected)
- Commitment outcome: syllabus sent Y/N, referral made Y/N
- One thing you will change before the next demo
