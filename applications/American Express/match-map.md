  build and the AI-assisted method, never a user base.
- **Agile / Scrum / sprint vocabulary.** Not required by this posting (unlike IBM), and forbidden by
  `skills-inventory.md` regardless. Absent.
- **"Product backlogs" as a named artifact.** The JD lists maintaining backlogs. No profile file
  evidences a maintained product backlog in a team setting, so no backlog claim is made; R5 is
  proved with the QA workflow and the decision log instead. **Interview note: if asked, describe
  STICK's slice sequencing honestly as a personal build, not a team backlog.**
- **"Develop business cases for new products, features and customer experiences" (R3).** Downgraded
  from the requirement table after review: nothing in the profile is an actual product business case
  (no cost/benefit framing, no revenue or adoption projection). The SMIF investment thesis and the
  Externship scoping are ADJACENT PROOF ONLY. No business-case bullet is on the resume. **This is
  the likeliest interview probe on the posting. Prepare a structured answer in advance rather than
  assuming it is covered.**
- **"Commercial" in the B2B sense.** Firmly is consumer e-commerce checkout; GCS serves business
  customers. R1 is satisfied on "payments" and "digital products," NOT on "commercial." Do not let
  the resume imply B2B payments experience. **Interview note: name the gap first and pivot to the
  Externship, which is the only real business-client engagement on the page.**
- **"Product backlogs" as a named artifact.** Unchanged from below.
- **Externship outcome claims.** The plan was delivered and well received; it has not been executed
  by the client. No engagement, follower, or reach numbers. Per `experience-externship.md`.
- **SMIF returns as portfolio performance.** The $400K AUM figure is fund size, not a return Veer
  produced. The bullets say "recommendation" and "report," never a realized gain.
- **MSME index precision.** Phrased as "flags which sectors are most exposed," not "ranks," per
  `project-msme-dashboard.md` (direction, not calibrated magnitude).

## Independent review, 2026-08-21

A fresh-context reviewer audited the draft against the research checklist, the full profile set,
this match-map, and the ATS extraction. Quantification share measured at 90% of bullets. No
inflation found against any profile "Would be INFLATION" block; the $400K AUM figure reads correctly
as fund size, not a return; no Agile vocabulary; no private-repo leak.

**APPLIED:**
- **Rendering defect.** The Leadership entry had been compressed to a running line to save space,
  which pushed the date range to break mid-range ("Aug. 2025 to Dec. / 2025") and severed it for an
  ATS. Restored to the standard entry macro and paid for the line by cutting filler from Education.
- **Floating skill claims.** "market and competitive research," usage telemetry, KPI dashboarding and
  Plotly were all listed in Skills with no supporting bullet on THIS resume, because the MSME bullet
  had been compressed. Dropped them. Skills now lists only what a bullet proves or what Veer can
  defend cold.
- **Externship never said what kind of plan it was.** "Marketing" appeared nowhere, though the JD
  names Marketing first among cross-functional partners. Now reads "communications and marketing
  plan," which is the profile's own wording.
- **SMIF gained the competitive-landscape and revenue-driver research** that this match-map claimed
  for R4 but the page did not actually contain.
- **MSME gained the public decision log** that this match-map claimed for R5 but the page did not
  contain, and softened "statistic" to "figure" to match the profile's hedge.
- **Firmly gained "UX" and "across time zones,"** both profile-backed, covering the cross-functional
  breadth in R6 and the time-zones preferred qual that had no proof.
- **Education bullet** was a verbless fragment that also fragmented the ATS extraction of the
  coursework list. Cut to the coursework line alone.
- **Entry-date placement** was inconsistent between the two Projects entries. Normalized.
